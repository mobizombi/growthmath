Free calculator widgets for your website

Add any GrowthMath calculator to your blog, course, newsletter landing page or client site - free, no signup, no ads inside the widget. Copy the code, paste it into an HTML block, done.

How to embed a calculator

  1. Pick a calculator below and click Embed this calculator.
  2. Copy the code.
  3. In WordPress, add a Custom HTML block and paste it. In Webflow, Ghost, Squarespace or Wix, use the "Embed" or "HTML" element.

The widget resizes itself to fit, works on mobile, and runs entirely in your visitor's browser - nothing they type is sent anywhere. If your platform strips scripts, the iframe still works; it just keeps a fixed height, so raise the height value if it gets cut off.

Terms

Widgets are free for any site, commercial or not. Please keep the credit line under the calculator and do not frame the widget inside a page that misrepresents what it does.
