Margin vs. markup

They use the same two numbers but divide by different things, which is why they are so often confused.

Margin = (Price - Cost) / Price
Markup = (Price - Cost) / Cost
Price for target margin = Cost / (1 - Target margin)

A product that costs $40 and sells for $100 has a 60% margin but a 150% markup. Pricing with "50% markup" when you meant "50% margin" leaves you with a 33% margin instead.

Frequently asked questions

Can margin be over 100%?

No. Margin is a share of the price, so it can approach but never reach 100%. Markup can be any size.

What should I include in cost?

For gross margin, include the direct cost of the unit: product, packaging, shipping to you, and payment fees. Rent and salaries belong in net margin.
