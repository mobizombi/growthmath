UTM Link Builder

Tag every link the same way, every time - so your GA4 reports stop splitting "Facebook", "facebook" and "fb" into three sources.

What each UTM parameter means

ParameterAnswersExample
utm_sourceWhere is the traffic from?facebook, google, newsletter
utm_mediumWhat type of channel?paid_social, cpc, email, affiliate
utm_campaignWhich campaign or promotion?spring_sale_2026
utm_contentWhich ad or link variant?video_a, blue_button
utm_termWhich keyword (search ads)?running_shoes

Rules that keep GA4 reports clean

  • Always lowercase. GA4 is case-sensitive - "Facebook" and "facebook" become two rows.
  • Pick one medium vocabulary and stick to it. GA4's default channel groups recognise values like cpc, email, social, paid_social, affiliate.
  • Never UTM-tag internal links on your own site - it restarts the session and overwrites the original source.
  • Keep a naming sheet so the whole team uses the same campaign names.

Whatever you choose, write it down in a shared sheet. The single most common analytics mess is three people tagging the same channel three different ways.

Google Ads and auto-tagging

If your Google Ads account is linked to GA4 with auto-tagging on, Google adds a gclid parameter and GA4 fills source, medium and campaign automatically. You usually do not need UTMs on Google Ads, and adding conflicting manual tags can cause confusion. Use UTMs for every other paid and unpaid channel.

Where to find UTM data in GA4

  • Reports - Acquisition - Traffic acquisition: sessions by session source / medium and session campaign.
  • Reports - Acquisition - User acquisition: the first source that brought each new user.
  • Add a secondary dimension of "Session manual ad content" to see utm_content variants side by side.

Mistakes that break attribution

  • Spaces in values. Use underscores or hyphens - spaces become %20 and look different from the same name with underscores.
  • Tagging links inside your own site, which starts a new session and overwrites the real source.
  • Using utm_source for the campaign name. Keep source as the place, campaign as the promotion.
  • Shorteners that drop parameters. Test the final URL after shortening.

Frequently asked questions

Which UTM parameters are required?

Source, medium and campaign should always be set. Content and term are optional.

Do UTM parameters hurt SEO?

No, as long as you only use them on external links (ads, emails, social posts). Search engines treat the canonical URL as the page.

Do UTM parameters work in emails?

Yes, and they should be on every link in marketing emails. Many email platforms can add them automatically - check that their naming matches your convention.

Are UTM parameters case-sensitive?

Yes. GA4 treats "Email" and "email" as different values. Keep everything lowercase.
