What is EPC?

EPC (earnings per click) is the average amount you earn every time someone clicks your affiliate link. It is the single best number for comparing offers, because it combines commission size and conversion rate into one figure.

EPC = Conversion rate x Payout per sale
Monthly earnings = Clicks x EPC

Why a 50% commission can lose to a 10% one

A 50% commission on a $20 product that converts at 1% earns $0.10 per click. A 10% commission on a $400 product that converts at 2% earns $0.80 per click - eight times more for the same traffic. Always compare EPC, not commission rate.

Working backwards from a goal

Divide your income goal by EPC to see the traffic you need. If $3,000/month needs 50,000 clicks, you know whether the plan is realistic - and whether to find a higher-EPC offer first.

Where to find real conversion rates

Most affiliate networks show network-wide EPC on the offer page. Use it as a starting estimate, then replace it with your own numbers after a few hundred clicks - your audience will convert differently.

Things EPC does not show

  • Recurring commissions. A subscription program that pays every month the customer stays is worth far more than its first-month EPC. Multiply by the average customer lifetime.
  • Cookie window. A 1-day cookie (Amazon) loses sales from people who come back later. A 30 to 90-day cookie captures them.
  • Reversals. Some programs reverse 10 to 30% of commissions for returns or cancelled trials. Use approved commissions, not pending ones.
  • Payout reliability. A high EPC from a program that pays late or changes terms is worth less than it looks.

How to raise your EPC

  • Send clicks from high-intent pages: comparisons, "best X for Y" and reviews convert far better than general articles.
  • Link to the specific product, not the homepage.
  • Negotiate: once you send consistent volume, ask the affiliate manager for a higher tier, a bonus or an exclusive discount code.
  • Test placement: a comparison table near the top of the page usually beats links at the bottom.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good EPC?

It depends on the niche. $0.10 - $0.50 is common for retail, while finance, software and dating offers often pay $1 - $5+ per click.

Is EPC shown per click or per 100 clicks?

Some networks (such as CJ) show EPC per 100 clicks. Divide by 100 to compare with this calculator.

How do I calculate EPC from my network report?

Divide total commissions by total clicks for the same period. $540 in commissions from 1,200 clicks is an EPC of $0.45.

Why is my EPC lower than the network says?

Network EPC is an average across all affiliates, including coupon and loyalty sites that convert very well. Content and social traffic usually converts lower. Trust your own numbers after a few hundred clicks.
