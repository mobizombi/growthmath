Affiliate Link Checker for Chrome

Click once on any page to see every affiliate link on it: which network, which tracking ID, whether it carries rel="sponsored", and whether the page has a disclosure. Free, no account, nothing leaves your browser.

Download for Chrome (v1.0.0)

Chrome Web Store listing coming soon. Until then, install it in 30 seconds with the steps below - it works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

What it checks

  • Every affiliate link, by network. Amazon Associates, Impact, CJ, Awin, Rakuten, ShareASale, ClickBank, eBay Partner Network, Partnerize, Skimlinks, Sovrn, AvantLink, FlexOffers, Digistore24, Booking.com, SaaS referral programs (Rewardful, FirstPromoter, PartnerStack) and more - plus cloaked links like /go/ and /recommends/.
  • Tracking IDs. See the Amazon tag, Impact or CJ publisher ID, Awin affiliate ID and others on every link. Catch a missing or wrong tag before it costs you commissions.
  • rel="sponsored". Google asks for paid and affiliate links to carry rel="sponsored" (or nofollow). Links without it are counted and outlined in red on the page.
  • Disclosure. Finds wording like "As an Amazon Associate" or "we may earn a commission", and warns you if it only appears after the first affiliate link.
  • Export to CSV for audits, client reports or competitor research.

Who it is for

  • Affiliate publishers and bloggers: audit your own pages for broken tags, missing rel attributes and disclosure placement.
  • Affiliate managers: check how partners link to you, and spot which networks and competitors a publisher already works with before you pitch them.
  • SEO agencies: run a link-attribute compliance check on a client's money pages in seconds.

How to install (while the Web Store listing is in review)

  1. Download the zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Pin the extension from the puzzle-piece menu, open any page and click the icon.

Privacy

The extension only reads a page when you click its icon, uses Chrome's activeTab permission (no access to your browsing otherwise), makes no network requests, stores nothing and contains no analytics. The source is a few small, readable files.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Affiliate Link Checker free?

Yes. It is free, needs no account, and has no paid tier.

Do affiliate links need rel="sponsored"?

Google asks that links you are paid for, including affiliate links, are marked rel="sponsored" or rel="nofollow". Unmarked paid links can be treated as a link scheme.

Does it detect every affiliate link?

It detects links by network domain, URL pattern and common affiliate parameters, which covers most programs. Links that hide behind an unrelated redirect domain cannot be identified without following them, so treat the count as a minimum.

Does the extension send my data anywhere?

No. It runs only when you click it, reads the current page locally, makes no network requests and stores nothing.
